# Project Specifications — Kolchi Dyalk

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** November 25, 2026  
**Team:** 5 members

## 1. Project Overview

Kolchi Dyalk is an all-in-one website that brings together local commerce, services, transportation, employment opportunities, events and citizen initiatives.

The project will initially launch in the municipality of Belfaa before gradually expanding to other municipalities and regions, and eventually across Morocco.

## 2. Problem Statement

The absence of a centralised digital platform in Belfaa leads to several difficulties:

- Difficulty accessing information about local businesses, services and transportation.
- Limited visibility for merchants, artisans and small businesses.
- Difficulty finding employment, internship and training opportunities.
- Lack of communication and citizen participation in local development.
- Information scattered across Facebook, WhatsApp and word of mouth.

## 3. Project Objectives

### 3.1. General Objective

Create a centralised website that facilitates access to local information and services and gradually contributes to the economic and social development of the region.

### 3.2. Specific Objectives

- Promote entrepreneurs, artisans, farmers and small businesses.
- Share job, internship and training opportunities.
- Encourage residents to participate in their community.
- Enable users to report local problems.
- Encourage mutual aid and community initiatives.
- Identify the needs of the local population.
- Promote the region's resources and opportunities.
- Facilitate access to information from a phone or computer.

## 4. Target Audience

- Local residents.
- Merchants, entrepreneurs, artisans and farmers.
- Cooperatives.
- Students, young people and job seekers.
- Associations and community organisations.
- Local authorities and public institutions.
- Visitors and tourists.

## 5. Website Features

### 5.1. Homepage

The homepage presents all Kolchi Dyalk services and provides easy access to the five website modules: Souk Local, Hirfa, Opportunities and Events, Transport and Sawtna.

### 5.2. Souk Local

This module helps users discover where to find and buy products in their region.

- Search for products by category.
- Display markets, shops, cooperatives and other points of sale.
- Display the exact locations of points of sale on a map.
- Provide practical information about places where products are sold.

**Examples:** Search for vegetables to find the vegetable market, fish to find the fish market, or argan oil to find cooperatives that sell it.

This module is a guide for finding products and points of sale. It does not include online ordering, payment or delivery.

### 5.3. Hirfa

This module helps users find local artisans and service providers, including plumbers, electricians, mechanics and painters.

Each professional has a profile containing:

- Name and profession.
- A short introduction to their work.
- Photos of their work or completed projects.
- Contact details.
- Geographic location on a map.

### 5.4. Opportunities and Events

This module brings together local opportunities and events.

- Job and internship opportunities.
- Training programmes and workshops.
- Cultural, sporting and social events.
- Information about dates, venues, organisers and their contact details.
- Search and filter announcements by category.

Businesses, associations and organisers contact the Kolchi Dyalk team to submit their announcements. The team verifies and publishes the announcements. Organisers cannot publish directly on the website.

### 5.5. Transport

This module facilitates access to information about local transportation.

- Available means of transport (taxis, buses, etc.).
- Available destinations and routes.
- Travel fares.
- Exact locations of transport stations on a map.
- Contact details for transport providers.

Schedules and departure times are not included in this version.

### 5.6. Sawtna (صوتنا)

Sawtna is a citizen participation space that combines reporting local problems with proposing ideas to improve the region.

When creating a post, the user chooses between two types: **Problem** or **Idea**.

#### Problems

- Submit a report with a title and description.
- Add photos of the problem.
- Specify the exact location of the problem on a map.
- Classify problems by category (roads, lighting, waste, water, etc.).
- Track the status: reported, being resolved or resolved.

#### Ideas

- Publish ideas and proposals to improve the region.
- Add a title, description and photos.
- Classify ideas by category (education, environment, economy, infrastructure, etc.).
- Track the progress of proposed initiatives.

#### Shared Features

- Comments and discussions on posts.
- Positive (Like) and negative (Dislike) votes.
- Rank posts by their voting scores to highlight the problems and ideas that receive the most support from residents.

This ranking aims to increase the visibility of the population's needs and encourage the resolution of problems and the implementation of citizen initiatives.

### 5.7. User Accounts

- Free access to the website and its modules without registration.
- An account is required to publish, comment, like or dislike posts on Sawtna.

### 5.8. Registration and Login

- Registration and login using an email address and password.
- Registration and login using a Google account.
- Forgotten password reset.

### 5.9. Administration Dashboard

The administration area allows the team to:

- Manage user accounts.
- Add, edit and delete content across the different modules.
- Moderate Sawtna posts and comments.
- Suspend or ban users who violate the website's rules.
- View website statistics.
- Manage administrators and their permissions.

### 5.10. Website Languages

Kolchi Dyalk will be available in four languages:

- French.
- Arabic.
- Tamazight (Tachelhit).
- English.

## 6. Team Organisation

The project is carried out by a team of five members, including a project manager.

The project manager is responsible for team coordination, monitoring project progress and administering Jira and GitHub.

Responsibilities will be divided by role:

- Frontend development.
- Backend development.
- Database management.
- Design.
- Testing.

Individual role assignments will be determined later by the team.

The selected collaboration tools are:

- **Jira:** Task management, work allocation and progress tracking.
- **GitHub:** Source code management and collaboration between team members.

## 7. Project Schedule

Development will cover the website as a whole, integrating the five modules defined in these project specifications.

The detailed schedule, deadlines and task assignments will be determined later by the team.
